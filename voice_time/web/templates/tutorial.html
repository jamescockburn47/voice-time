🎓 Tutorial & Testing Guide

Learn how to use Voice Time with realistic examples. Try these scenarios to familiarize yourself with the system.

Voice Mode: Press and hold Ctrl+Space to talk

Or type your commands in the Dashboard

1

Morning Planning

Start your day by telling the system what you plan to work on. It will structure your tasks automatically.

  • Today I need to review the Thompson witness statements, draft the Greenfield skeleton, and I've got a conference at 3
  • Working on Thompson and Williams today, plus various emails
  • Need to finish the acquisition documents for TechGrowth and research planning law for Greenfield
Try It Now →
2

Starting Work

Tell the system when you begin working on something. It starts tracking time automatically.

  • Working on Thompson witness statements
  • Starting the Greenfield research on planning law
  • Beginning the Williams correspondence
  • Moving to the TechGrowth acquisition documents
Try It Now →
3

Completing Tasks

When finished, the system infers duration and creates a professional billing narrative.

  • Done with the Thompson witnesses
  • Finished the research - took about 90 minutes
  • Completed the drafting, all morning on it
  • Done with that
Try It Now →
4

Handling Interruptions

Seamlessly switch between tasks. The system remembers what you were doing and can return to it.

  • Quick call about Williams employment matter
  • Back to what I was doing
  • Brief email to Thompson client then back to drafting
  • Urgent - need to review Chen PI medical report
Try It Now →
5

Natural Duration Language

Use everyday language to describe time. The system understands natural phrases.

  • Quick 10 minute call with Greenfield client
  • Spent a couple of hours on the Thompson drafting
  • All morning reviewing the disclosure bundle
  • Since lunch I've been on the acquisition documents
  • About an hour and a half researching case law
Try It Now →
6

Historical Logging

Log work you already completed but forgot to record at the time.

  • Spent 30 minutes on Williams emails this morning
  • Was on the Thompson call for about an hour earlier
  • Did a couple of hours research for Greenfield yesterday
  • Forgot to log - spent all afternoon on the Chen medical report
Try It Now →
