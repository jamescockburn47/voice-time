How TimeBrief Works

Traditional time tracking tools make you click through menus and type descriptions. We let you just speak.

Most legal time tools ask: "What do you want to record?" We ask: "What are you working on?" Say it naturally. Our AI understands your intent, matches the matter, and starts the timer. No menus. No typing. Just work.

Cutting-Edge AI, Running Locally

Unlike cloud-based tools, TimeBrief runs entirely on your machine. Your voice never leaves your computer. Your data stays yours.

Smart Hardware Detection

TimeBrief automatically detects your hardware - NVIDIA GPU, AMD Ryzen AI, or standard CPU - and downloads the optimal AI models for your specific machine. Powerful laptop? Get larger, more accurate models. Modest hardware? Get lightweight models that still deliver.

No configuration needed. The right models are selected and downloaded automatically on first run.

Voice-First Intelligence

State-of-the-art speech recognition (Whisper) transcribes your voice locally. No internet required.

Intent Recognition

Local LLM understands what you mean. Say "working on the Smith case" and it knows exactly which matter.

100% Local Processing

Your confidential client information never touches the cloud. Everything runs on your hardware.

Hardware-Optimized

GPU acceleration on NVIDIA (CUDA), int8 optimization on AMD Ryzen AI, and efficient CPU fallback for any machine.

Four Things We Do Differently

1

Voice as Primary Input

Lawyers talk all day. Why force them to type? Speak naturally and the AI handles the rest. Ctrl+Space to talk, release to process.

2

Plan-Aware Recognition

Plan your day in the morning. When you say "Smith letter" later, the system knows you mean that planned task and starts with all details pre-filled.

3

Intelligent Clarification

If it's not sure, it asks. "What type of work?" "Which matter?" Natural conversation, not error messages.

4

6-Minute Billing Units

Industry-standard billing increments built in. See units accumulate in real-time. No manual calculation needed.

The Three-Step Flow

1

Plan Your Day

Add tasks by voice or form. "Write letter on Smith, research for Brown, call on Thompson." Tasks become vocabulary hints for better recognition.

2

Work and Speak

When you start a task, just say it. "Working on Smith letter." Timer starts with matter, activity, and description already filled in.

3

Review and Export

End of day, review entries by voice. "Change entry 3 to 30 minutes." Export to your practice management system.
