🧠 Context Awareness

Describe your day in the morning. Throughout the day, just say "done with that" and the system knows exactly what you mean. No clicking through dropdowns, no re-entering matter names.

"Couple of hours", "all morning", "since lunch" - the system understands natural language. Infers duration from context. No manual timer management required.

"Quick call about Brown" then "back to what I was doing" - task stack remembers your context. Handle interruptions naturally without losing track.

📝 Narrative Generation

Technical Innovations

🔒

Absolute Privacy

Everything runs locally. Voice processing, AI inference, all data - stays on your machine. No cloud, no third parties, no data breaches. Client confidentiality guaranteed.

⚡

GPU Acceleration

Auto-detects your hardware. NVIDIA CUDA for RTX GPUs, optimized int8 for AMD Ryzen, intelligent fallback to CPU. Sub-200ms transcription on modern laptops.

🎯

Fast Path Architecture

80% of during-day commands process without LLM using heuristics and fuzzy matching. Sub-second response. LLM only for complex planning and narrative generation.

🧠

Small, Fast LLM

Qwen 1.5B runs locally on CPU. Excellent JSON parsing for plans, professional narrative generation. 200-400ms inference. No OpenAI API, no internet required.

📊

Recency Weighting

Matters used today score 2x higher in matching. "Smith" resolves to the Smith you're actually working on, not the Smith from last month. Context-aware intelligence.

🎙️

Hotkey Activation

Press Ctrl+Space to talk. No wake words, no constant listening, no privacy concerns. Professional, reliable, and respects your privacy. Works in browser or globally.

How It Works

Morning (9:00 AM)

"Today I need to finish the Thompson disclosure, draft the Greenfield skeleton, and I've got a call with counsel at 3"

→ System parses into 3 structured tasks across 2 matters

During Day (10:30 AM)

"Done with the disclosure"

→ Infers 1.5 hours, generates "Review and analysis of disclosure bundle", logs to Thompson

End of Day (5:00 PM)

Review entries with voice commands. "Change entry 2 to 3 hours." Export to CSV. Done in 60 seconds instead of 30 minutes.
